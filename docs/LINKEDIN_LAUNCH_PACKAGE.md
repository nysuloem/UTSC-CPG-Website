### About

The UTSC Computational Physiology Group brings together undergraduate researchers, educators, and collaborators at the University of Toronto Scarborough to advance whole-body mathematical modelling with HumMod.

Our students work across reproductive, cardiovascular, renal, metabolic, neural, endocrine, and musculoskeletal physiology. They learn to turn physiological questions into model structure, simulations, validation, and scientific communication.

A central goal of the group is to help physiological models better represent variation across sex, age, physiology, and population contexts. We also develop scalable ways to make meaningful computational research experiences available to undergraduate students.

Follow the Page for project updates, research outputs, student achievements, HumMod release information, and opportunities to join or collaborate.

HumMod is exclusively licensed to HC Simulation, LLC.

### Post 2 — What is HumMod?

Physiology is a network, not a list of organs.

HumMod is an integrative mathematical model with thousands of time-dependent relationships across the human body. Change one condition, run time forward, and observe how effects propagate across systems.

At UTSC, we use HumMod as both a research environment and a teaching platform. It gives students a safe, quantitative way to test ideas—and a rigorous framework for connecting equations to evidence.
